# Navigating Ambiguity

Senior roles expect you to make progress when requirements are unclear.

## Approach

1. **Clarify the goal** and the decision the work informs.
2. **Frame options** with rough cost/risk and pick a default.
3. **Time-box** exploration; set checkpoints and kill criteria.
4. **Communicate** assumptions and invite correction early.
5. **Deliver** a useful increment, then refine with feedback.

## Signals interviewers want

- You ask sharp questions instead of waiting to be told.
- You distinguish reversible from irreversible decisions.
- You make assumptions explicit and validate them cheaply.
- You keep stakeholders aligned through frequent updates.

## Example prompts

- "Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete data."
- "Describe a project where the requirements changed mid-flight."
- "How do you prioritize when everything is urgent?"
