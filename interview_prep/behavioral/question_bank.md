# Behavioral Question Bank

## Teamwork and conflict

- Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate. How did you resolve it?
- Describe a time you received difficult feedback.
- How do you handle a teammate who is not pulling their weight?

## Failure and growth

- Tell me about your biggest professional failure.
- Describe a time you made a wrong technical decision.
- What is the hardest bug you have debugged?

## Leadership and influence

- Tell me about a time you led without authority.
- How did you convince a skeptical stakeholder?
- Describe mentoring someone.

## Ambiguity and prioritization

- Tell me about a project with unclear requirements.
- How do you prioritize competing deadlines?
- Describe a time you had to say no.

## Impact and ownership

- What is the project you are most proud of, and why?
- Tell me about a time you exceeded expectations.
- How have you improved reliability or performance measurably?

## Craft

- How do you ensure quality under deadline pressure?
- Describe a time you simplified a complex system.
- How do you keep learning new technologies?

## Preparation tip

Write a one-page "story bank" mapping each question to one of 6-8 STAR
stories, with the metric and the lesson.
