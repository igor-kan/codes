# Nuclear & Particle Physics

Nuclear physics, relativistic kinematics, and quantum decay algorithms based on:
- **Kenneth S. Krane**: *Introductory Nuclear Physics* (Wiley)
- **Kenneth S. Krane**: *Modern Physics*
- **Halliday, Resnick, Krane**: *Physics* (Vol. 1 & 2)

Features Bethe-Weizsäcker Semi-Empirical Mass Formula (SEMF), Bateman radioactive decay chain solvers, Gamow alpha decay barrier tunneling, relativistic Compton scattering, longitudinal/transverse relativistic Doppler shifts, Rutherford differential cross sections, reaction threshold kinematics, Bethe-Bloch stopping power and Bragg peak models, Mössbauer recoil energies, pair production thresholds, nuclear shell model magic numbers, relativistic invariant mass reconstruction, nuclear reaction Q-values, and accelerator gyroradii.
