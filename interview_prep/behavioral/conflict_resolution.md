# Conflict Resolution

Interviewers want evidence you can disagree without damaging relationships.

## Framework

1. **Understand** the other position by restating it in their terms.
2. **Separate** people from the problem; focus on shared goals and data.
3. **Generate** options and evaluate trade-offs objectively.
4. **Decide** via agreed criteria; escalate only if blocked.
5. **Commit** publicly once decided, even if you disagreed.

## Strong signals

- Golden-rule empathy and low ego.
- Using data or a spike to resolve debates instead of seniority.
- Knowing when to disagree and commit.
- Repairing the relationship afterward.

## Anti-patterns

- Winning the argument and losing the teammate.
- Avoiding the conflict passively and letting it fester.
- Escalating before trying to resolve directly.

## Sample prompt

"Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager or a senior engineer."
