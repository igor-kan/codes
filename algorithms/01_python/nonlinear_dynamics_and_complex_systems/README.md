# Nonlinear Dynamics & Complex Systems

Algorithms and simulations inspired by Neil Gershenfeld's *The Nature of Mathematical Modeling*.
Covers Takens' delay embedding, Lyapunov exponents, Poincaré sections, Feigenbaum bifurcation cascade, Hénon & Rössler attractors, Grassberger-Procaccia correlation dimension, 1D cellular automata (Wolfram rules), percolation, BTW abelian sandpiles, and Kuramoto phase synchronization.
