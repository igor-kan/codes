# Leadership and Ownership

Stories should show you **raising the bar**, not just executing tickets.

## Themes interviewers probe

- **Ownership:** you went beyond your job description.
- **Customer obsession:** decisions anchored to user impact.
- **Bias for action:** shipping with incomplete information, then iterating.
- **Dive deep:** debugging to root cause instead of patching symptoms.
- **Insist on high standards:** raising the quality bar for the team.
- **Disagree and commit:** advocating, then supporting the decision.
- **Deliver results:** measurable outcomes despite obstacles.
- **Hire and develop the best:** mentoring, feedback, onboarding.
- **Think big:** connecting work to long-term strategy.
- **Earn trust:** transparency, admitting mistakes, following through.
