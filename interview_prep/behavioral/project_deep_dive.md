# Project Deep Dive

Expect 15-30 minutes on your resume's most impressive project. Be ready to go
from a one-minute summary to the lowest level of detail.

## Outline

1. **Context:** users, scale, constraints, why it mattered.
2. **Requirements:** functional, non-functional (latency, cost, availability).
3. **Architecture:** components, data flow, key decisions and alternatives.
4. **Your role:** exactly what you designed, built, reviewed, or operated.
5. **Hard problems:** the trickiest bug, scaling wall, or trade-off.
6. **Metrics:** before/after numbers; business impact.
7. **What you'd change:** with hindsight or more time.

## Be ready for follow-ups

- Why this database/broker/protocol over alternatives?
- How did you handle failure, retries, and consistency?
- How would it change at 10x or 100x scale?
- What did you measure, and how?
- What was the cost and operational burden?

## Red flags to avoid

- Vague ownership ("we built") without your specific contribution.
- No numbers and no trade-offs.
- Claiming a design you cannot defend under questioning.
