# Failure Stories

Prepare 2-3 genuine failures with clear ownership and learning.

## Structure

- What you were trying to do and why it mattered.
- The mistake *you* made (not bad luck or others).
- Immediate impact and how you contained it.
- Root cause and the systemic fix.
- What you changed in your own behavior since.

## Choose failures that show growth

- A technical decision that backfired (over-engineering, wrong abstraction).
- A missed deadline due to underestimated scope.
- A production incident you caused and helped resolve.
- A people/communication failure you repaired.

## Avoid

- "I work too hard" or humble-brag pseudo-failures.
- Blaming teammates, processes, or the org.
- Failures with no consequence (nothing to learn).
- Rehashing something you clearly did not own.

## Close with evidence

State the measurable change: a design review practice, a rollback process, a
checklist, or a new test that prevents recurrence.
