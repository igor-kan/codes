# Microservices

Small, independently deployable services aligned to business capabilities, each
owning its data.

## Enablers

- Automated CI/CD, observability, and service discovery.
- API gateways/BFFs and service mesh.
- Team autonomy and clear ownership (Conway's Law).

## Costs

- Distributed data, eventual consistency, and sagas.
- Network latency, partial failure, and operational overhead.

Start with a modular monolith and extract services when the pressure is real.

Related: Modular Monolith, Microservices Patterns, DDD.
