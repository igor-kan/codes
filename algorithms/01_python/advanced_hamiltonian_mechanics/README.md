# Advanced Hamiltonian & Geometric Mechanics

Geometric mechanics implementations drawn from foundational literature:
- **V. I. Arnold**: *Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics* (Springer)
- **Landau & Lifshitz**: *Mechanics* (Course of Theoretical Physics, Vol. 1)

Features canonical symplectic forms, Lie brackets and derivatives of vector fields, Arnold's cat map and Lyapunov exponents, Euler-Poinsot rigid body dynamics, Kovalevskaya top invariants, Lagrange top nutation, Hénon-Heiles non-integrable Hamiltonian system, Birkhoff normal forms, Lie series perturbation theory, Whitham averaged Lagrangians, Floquet monodromy matrices, Marsden-Weinstein symplectic reduction on SO(3), Calogero-Moser Lax pairs, Hannay geometric angles, and Chirikov standard twist maps.
