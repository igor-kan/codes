# Continuum & Fluid Mechanics

Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and continuum mechanics algorithms based on:
- **Landau & Lifshitz**: *Fluid Mechanics* (Course of Theoretical Physics, Vol. 6)
- **Kincaid & Cheney**: *Numerical Analysis: Mathematics of Scientific Computing*

Features Chorin's projection method for 2D incompressible Navier-Stokes, Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM D2Q9), vorticity-streamfunction cavity solvers, 1D Sod shock tube compressible Euler solvers, Rankine-Hugoniot oblique shock relations, Blasius boundary layer shooting solver, 1D nonlinear shallow water equations, viscous Burgers' equation solvers, Poiseuille-Couette flows, Biot-Savart vortex filament dynamics, Rayleigh-Bénard convection, Kelvin-Helmholtz instabilities, 2D Helmholtz-Hodge decompositions, aerodynamic point-vortex potential flows, and Airy surface gravity wave dispersion.
