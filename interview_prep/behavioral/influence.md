# Influence Without Authority

Getting work done through others when you have no direct authority.

## Tactics

- **Lead with the problem**, not your solution.
- **Speak their language:** tie proposals to the other team's goals and metrics.
- **Bring data and a prototype**, not opinions.
- **Reduce their cost:** do the integration work, provide a migration path.
- **Build allies** early; socialize ideas before formal review.
- **Pick battles:** spend credibility on what matters.

## Story ingredients

- A cross-team dependency you unblocked.
- A standard or migration you convinced others to adopt.
- A decision you reversed by presenting new evidence.

## Anti-patterns

- Escalation as a first resort.
- Framing it as winning a debate.
- Ignoring the other team's constraints and incentives.

## Sample prompt

"Tell me about a time you convinced a team to adopt your idea."
