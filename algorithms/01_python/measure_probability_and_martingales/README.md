# Measure-Theoretic Probability & Martingales

Algorithms and simulations inspired by Michael Evans & Jeffrey Rosenthal, *Probability and Statistics: The Science of Uncertainty*.
Covers Doob martingale convergence, Radon-Nikodym derivatives, Markov chain mixing times, Coupling From The Past (CFTP), Poisson point processes, Brownian motion quadratic variation, Itô isometry, Stratonovich integrals, Chernoff-Cramer bounds, and Fokker-Planck equations.
