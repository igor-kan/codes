# Technical References & Bibliography: codes

This document compiles the foundational literature, academic papers, and technical standards underpinning the implementations in `codes`.

## Foundational Literature

1. **Golub, G. H., & Van Loan, C. F.** (2013). *Matrix Computations* (4th ed.). Johns Hopkins University Press.
2. **Knuth, D. E.** (1997). *The Art of Computer Programming, Volume 2: Seminumerical Algorithms* (3rd ed.). Addison-Wesley.
3. **Wilkinson, J. H.** (1994). *Rounding Errors in Algebraic Processes*. Dover Publications.
4. **Hennessy, J. L., & Patterson, D. A.** (2019). *Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach* (6th ed.). Morgan Kaufmann.
5. **Higham, N. J.** (2002). *Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms* (2nd ed.). SIAM.

## Standards and Specifications

- **IEEE Standard 754-2019**: *IEEE Standard for Floating-Point Arithmetic*.
- **ISO/IEC 9899:2018 (C18)** & **ISO/IEC 14882:2020 (C++20)**: Programming Language Standards.
- **OpenMP Application Program Interface Version 5.2** (November 2021).
- **PCI-SIG & JEDEC**: Memory and Cacheline Hierarchy Standards.
