# Astrophysics & Stellar Dynamics

Astrophysical algorithms and numerical astrophysics models based on:
- **Bradley W. Carroll & Dale A. Ostlie**: *An Introduction to Modern Astrophysics* (Cambridge)

Features Kepler orbit equation solvers, Lane-Emden polytropic interior solvers, Planck blackbody spectra, Saha ionization equilibria, Eggleton Roche-lobe geometries, Chandrasekhar white dwarf mass limits, Jeans mass collapse conditions, Oort galactic constants, pulsar dispersion delays, Friedmann cosmological expansion, Hohmann transfer orbits, and cosmological luminosity distance metrics.
