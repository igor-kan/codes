# Classical Electrodynamics & Field Theory

Algorithms and field-theoretic models based on:
- **Landau & Lifshitz**: *The Classical Theory of Fields* (Vol. 2)
- **Landau & Lifshitz**: *Electrodynamics of Continuous Media* (Vol. 8)
- **Arfken, Weber, Harris**: *Mathematical Methods for Physicists*

Contains implementations of Liénard-Wiechert potentials, synchrotron radiation, Lorentz field transformations, Poynting vectors and Maxwell stress tensors, rectangular waveguide modes, multipole expansions, Biot-Savart integrators, Rayleigh/Mie scattering, Cherenkov radiation, bremsstrahlung spectra, guiding-center plasma drifts, dipole radiation, skin depth, Dirac monopoles, and the relativistic Boris particle pusher.
