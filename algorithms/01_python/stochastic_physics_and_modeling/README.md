# Stochastic Physics & Mathematical Modeling

Implementations of stochastic processes and complex systems modeling based on:
- **Evans & Rosenthal**: *Probability and Random Processes*
- **Gershenfeld**: *The Nature of Mathematical Modeling* (Cambridge)
- **Landau & Lifshitz**: *Statistical Physics* (Course of Theoretical Physics, Vol. 5)

Features Langevin dynamics, Fokker-Planck finite-difference solvers, Gillespie SSA algorithms, CTMC master equation solvers, 2D Ising model Metropolis sweeps, Ornstein-Uhlenbeck processes, Lévy flights, fractional Brownian motion, MCMC Gelman-Rubin diagnostics, stochastic resonance, 2D percolation thresholds, flashing Brownian ratchets, Kramers escape rates, colored 1/f^alpha noise generators, and maximum entropy distribution estimators.
