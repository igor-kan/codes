# The STAR Method

Answer behavioral questions with a structured story:

- **S**ituation: context and constraints (1-2 sentences).
- **T**ask: your specific responsibility and goal.
- **A**ction: what *you* did, and why (bulk of the answer).
- **R**esult: measurable outcome, plus what you learned.

## Tips

- Keep it 1-2 minutes; lead with the result if the interviewer seems pressed.
- Use "I", not "we", when describing your contribution.
- Quantify: latency, revenue, users, incident minutes, adoption.
- Prepare 6-8 stories and remix them across questions (leadership, conflict,
  failure, ambiguity, influence, ownership, impact).

## Example skeleton

> At [company], our [system] had [problem] affecting [metric].
> I owned [scope]. I [action 1], [action 2], and coordinated [stakeholders].
> As a result, [metric improved] by [amount], and we [follow-up].
